# A Little Poem

The stars above shine bright and clear,
Their gentle light draws wanderers near.
The wind hums soft through rustling trees,
A quiet song upon the breeze.

The moon reflects on still, dark water,
Each ripple writ like nature's charter.
And in the hush of fading day,
The world breathes out its cares away.

So rest, dear heart, and close your eyes,
Beneath the vast and watching skies.
For in this moment, still and free,
The world is just where it should be.
