# Mew

A whisper in the morning light,
A gentle sound, a soft delight,
Mew, the name that floats like air,
A melody beyond compare.

Small paws that dance on moonlit floors,
Through open windows, endless doors,
Eyes that hold the starry night,
A tiny spark of pure delight.

So here's to Mew, forever bright,
A little wonder, shining light,
In every sound, in every hue,
The world is sweeter, thanks to you.
